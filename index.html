The thesis

Find the structure. Build it exactly.

Every computational problem has mathematical structure in it. We study it until we can state it precisely, then build it directly into small, deterministic systems. The work runs from theory, through experiment, to real hardware.

Flynn, our first graduate.

Flynn graduated from the lab in 2026.

FlynnComm · Graduated 2026 · Equipment health

Flynn

An equipment-health monitor developed in the lab. Its 8,480-byte firmware runs directly on the machine it watches and learns what healthy looks like for that machine.

Reported resultOn NASA's IMS bearing dataset, Flynn flagged developing faults about 17 days before failure and ran a 120-hour test with zero false positives. FlynnComm is taking it to market.
