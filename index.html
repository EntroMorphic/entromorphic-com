The thesis

Find the structure. Build it exactly.

Every hard problem has structure in it. We research until we find it, then build it directly into small, deterministic systems: ternary signatures, lookup tables, a few kilobytes of C. The result runs on a microcontroller, gives the same answer every time, and holds up in front of an auditor.

Flynn, our first graduate.

Flynn left the lab in 2026 and now operates as FlynnComm.

FlynnComm · Graduated 2026 · Equipment health

Flynn

An equipment-health monitor in 8,480 bytes of C99. It learns the machine it watches, locks its threshold, and runs bit-identically on any silicon, entirely on the device. FlynnComm is in discussions with industrial and aerospace buyers.

Why it graduatedBuyers can check every claim themselves. On the NASA IMS bearing dataset it ran 120 hours with zero false positives and called failures about 17 days early. It is deterministic by design and built for DO-178C and IEC 61508 certification.
