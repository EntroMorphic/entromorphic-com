What graduates from the lab.

When a validated artifact meets real demand, it becomes a standalone company with its own domain and team. Here is what has graduated so far.

FlynnComm · Graduated 2026 · Equipment health

Flynn

A single-file C99 equipment-health monitor in 8,480 bytes. It enrolls itself on the machine it watches, locks its threshold, and runs bit-identically on any silicon, entirely on the device. FlynnComm is in discussions with industrial and aerospace buyers.

Why it graduatedBuyers can verify it themselves. It is validated on the NASA IMS bearing dataset, deterministic by design, and built for the certification regimes of safety-critical industries (DO-178C, IEC 61508). That made it ready to run as a company of its own.
