Thesis

The research position.

The systems that run the physical world, from aircraft to industrial equipment to medical devices, need machine intelligence that is deterministic, auditable, and small enough for the hardware they already carry.

EntroMorphic builds that intelligence. We research methods that make on-device inference fit in kilobytes, run on microcontrollers and stand up to certification, and we prove each result by shipping it in a product.

Methodology

The research-to-spinout lifecycle.

Every project moves through four stages. A project that stalls at a stage is retired.

  1. 01

    Research

    We take a hard, specific problem in constrained or safety-critical compute and fund it through paid sponsorship or open-source work. The output is understanding, published openly where possible.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Understanding becomes an artifact: a working system the lab can measure and stand behind. To advance, it must be small and deterministic.

  3. 03

    Validation

    The prototype is hardened against the real deployment surface and independently verified. This is where a research result becomes a defensible technical claim.

  4. 04

    Graduation

    A validated artifact with real demand becomes a standalone company with its own domain and team. The lab keeps provenance and a stake.

8,480-byte equipment-health monitor
