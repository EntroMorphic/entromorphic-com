Research

The thesis, and the method.

Why the work exists, and how a project earns its way out of the lab.

Thesis

Entropy is structure.

Hard problems carry structure, including the parts that look like noise. We study it until we can state it precisely.

Then we build it directly: small, exact and deterministic. Built that way, machine intelligence runs on the hardware already in the field and gives answers an auditor can trace.

Method

From hypothesis to working system.

Every project moves through four stages, and each stage ends at a gate. A project that stalls at a gate is retired.

  1. 01

    Research

    We start from a hard, specific problem in constrained or safety-critical compute and state a testable hypothesis about its structure. Sponsors fund the work, or we do it in the open.

    GateA hypothesis precise enough to fail.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    The hypothesis becomes working code or hardware, with its performance measured on the hardware it targets.

    GateIt beats the null baseline.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Results are reproduced on real silicon and verified independently where possible. This is where a research result becomes a claim someone can rely on.

    GateA number the lab will put its name to.

  4. 04

    Commercialize

    Validated work with real demand leaves the lab through licensing, partnership, or a company of its own. The lab keeps provenance and a stake.

    GateDemand from outside the lab. Flynn was the first through.

Standards

How the lab keeps itself honest.

Four rules apply to every number the lab publishes.

STANDARD 01

Null baselines are mandatory.

Every report includes the simplest method that could explain its result, measured the same way. The rule dates from a celebrated 98.68% result that a zero-parameter identity function matched exactly.

STANDARD 02

Numbers travel with their scope.

The dataset, the configuration and the hardware go wherever a result goes. A claim is only as good as the conditions attached to it.

STANDARD 03

Determinism is tested.

Same input, same output, bit for bit, on the hardware a system targets. The lab checks it on that hardware.

STANDARD 04

Failures stay in the record.

Ideas that underperform are reported alongside the ones that work. A research record with only good news is a sales brochure.
