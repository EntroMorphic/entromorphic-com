The model

How the lab is funded, and what it keeps.

Paid research
Sponsors fund investigation into problems relevant to them, with a front-row seat to results and first look when work graduates.
Open-source research
Foundational work is published in public: determinism tooling, benchmarks, and reference implementations anyone can check.
Spinout thesis
Validated artifacts with real demand emit as standalone companies, each with its own domain and identity. Flynn is the first.
Retained stake
The lab keeps provenance and an ownership stake in what graduates, so the lab succeeds when its companies do.
