The model

How the lab is funded, and what it keeps.

Sponsored research
Sponsors fund work on their problems and see results as they're measured. They get first look at anything that graduates.
Open research
Foundational work is published in public, as code anyone can run and check.
Commercialization
Validated work with demand from outside the lab leaves through licensing, partnership, or a company of its own. Flynn is the first.
Retained stake
The lab keeps provenance and a stake in what graduates, so the lab succeeds when its companies do.
